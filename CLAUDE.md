# Campaign Command Center

Static campaign dashboard for True Lean Solutions / TechTranspire, published to GitHub Pages and refreshed daily from Zoho CRM.

## Layout
- `site/boxbyte.js` (`window.__BOXBYTE__`): the latest Box Byte batch file (datasets `boxbyte_leads`, `boxbyte_messages`, `boxbyte_held`), kept on the orphan `boxbyte-snapshot` branch and copied in by the workflow. The data layer merges its datasets into `data.js`'s.
- `site/courier.js` (`window.__COURIER__`): the Medical Courier prospects sheet (datasets `courier_sends`, `courier_file`), kept on the orphan `courier-snapshot` branch and copied in by the workflow. Company names and outreach flags only.
- `site/research.js` (`window.__RESEARCH__`): dataset `wi_research`, decision makers found by web research for target accounts with no other contact in Zoho (name, title, company, public LinkedIn URL, source, source date, confidence). Kept on the orphan `research-snapshot` branch and copied in by the workflow; the page treats it as empty when missing. A Zoho contact with the same name at the same account replaces the research entry.
- `scripts/fetch_zoho.py`: pulls the datasets from Zoho CRM via COQL (`/crm/v7/coql`) and writes `site/data.js` (`window.__DASH_DATA__ = {...}`, loads from file:// and Pages alike). Python standard library only; no dependencies.
- `site/index.html`: the whole page (styles, data layer, charts). d3 v7.9.0 comes from cdnjs; everything else is inline.
- `.github/workflows/refresh.yml`: runs the fetch at 12:30 UTC (6:00 PM IST), on push to `main` and on demand, then deploys `site/` with `actions/deploy-pages`.

## Commands
- Fetch data: `python3 scripts/fetch_zoho.py` (needs `ZOHO_CLIENT_ID`, `ZOHO_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ZOHO_REFRESH_TOKEN`)
- Serve locally: `python3 -m http.server --directory site 8000`

## How the page is built
- The page code talks to a small `window.dash` data layer at the top of `index.html`: `dash.loader` turns each raw Zoho answer into rows, `dash.calc` derives values from datasets, `dash.data(id)` reads them, and `dash.onData(fn)` redraws.
- Dataset IDs in `QUERIES` (fetch script) must match the `dash.loader(...)` IDs in the page.
- Campaign membership: B2B tag `May 2026 - 100 B2B`, ICP tag `2026ICP` (contacts and accounts), Founder Network by Relationship Type, Medical Courier account tag `2026MedicalCourier`. Priority 9/11 excluded everywhere; Founder Network wins over tags. The tag constants live in `fetch_zoho.py` and `CC_RULES` in the page; keep them in step. Deals are sorted by Deal Type (`CC_RULES.dealKind`). Campaign tabs and the Overview's monthly bars count the open pipeline only (Identify, Discovery, Proposals, Hold; `CC_RULES.isOpen`); B2B leaves out lost agreements but keeps signed ones (`CC_RULES.notLost`). The Pipeline tab shows every deal.
- Backlog: each campaign tab has a Backlog card; the Overview opens with a roll-up of B2B, ICP and Founder Network (Medical Courier stays on its own tab) (`dash.calc('backlog')`, labels in `BL_TEXT`). Agreements come from Zoho Sign (`sign_docs`: referral agreements and `Akhani_LLC_MNDA`): a party is Signed once any copy is signed, otherwise the newest non-recalled, non-draft copy decides; Tech Transpire/True Lean test sends are ignored. Overdue tasks are credited to their contact's campaign. A deal is stale when open and not updated for 14+ days (`STALE_DAYS`). Courier send counts come from the prospects sheet. Every backlog item opens (Clear ones too), each count is the length of the list it opens (`rp_backlog_people`, `icp_backlog_people`, `fn_backlog_people`, `mc_account_list`), and drill tables link names to the Zoho record via the sixth column entry (`[module, idField]`, see `zohoUrl`).
- Deals tab (`drawDeals`): filtered by Deal Type chips (`dlKind`), board columns Proposals, Discovery, Hold, Identify, then Closed Won, Closed Lost, Closed Lost to Competition. Age is days since `Modified_Time` (CT); stale is 14+ days (`DL_STALE`). Amounts show only where set (`amount_known`), so totals are floors. Loss reasons come from `Reason_For_Loss__s` on the `deals` query.
- ICP tab analysis (`drawIcp`, `icData`): verdict, next steps, target math, a ranked "Who to ask for a meeting" list (connected, never met; scored on Priority, seniority, ICP fit, size, connected colleagues; problem-first opener per industry group in `IC_OPENER`), acceptance by segment, ICP fit of accounts (`IC_IND`, `IC_PARTNER`) and account coverage flags. Built from `icp_backlog_people` (Connected, Requested, Email Required, plus P1/P3 not requested) and `icp_account_list`. New-request queue caps each account at `IC_MAX_PER_ACCOUNT`.
- Warm Intros tab (`#wi`; model `WI` and calcs `warm_paths`, `warm_people`; UI `wiDraw`): one row per target account (ICP and Medical Courier by tag, plus B2B: the companies of B2B campaign contacts whose firm hasn't signed our referral agreement; deduped by account ID) with every way in Zoho records. Levels, strongest first: 1 Same company (a relationship contact linked to the account), 2 Already in (a campaign contact there who accepted or met us), 3 Industry peer (same `CC_IND` group; always Unverified), 4 Recorded introduction (Referred By on a deal at the account). Confidence is separate: Verified (meeting, accepted connection, signed agreement, won client deal or referral recorded), Supported (link only), Unverified (Client label without a won deal, a same-name account on another record, or industry alone). Usable paths rank before unverified ones, then by level. Same-name accounts (`CC_NAMEKEY`) are flagged, never joined. A Client-labeled contact tagged 2026ICP, or at a Medical Courier target, is a campaign contact, not an introducer; so is a B2B-tagged contact at a B2B-only target. Built from `wi_introducers` (every contact with a Relationship Type), `wi_b2b_people` (B2B campaign contacts), `wi_icp_people` (every ICP campaign contact, for the person to reach) plus the ICP/MC account lists, `mc_contacts`, `deals_tagged`, `referral_links` and `agreements`. Person to reach: on a B2B-only account reached through a connected contact, that contact; otherwise the most senior contact there not yet met, other than the introducer; when Zoho has none, the web-research person (`wi_research`), whose name links to LinkedIn (`wiReach`). ICP's ask cards and Account coverage rows carry a Warm path badge (`wiBadge`) that opens the account here. Requests sent and introductions accepted aren't in Zoho, so the page doesn't show them; the proposed Introductions module is described on the tab. Industry groups (`CC_IND`) are shared with the ICP tab.
- Every number on the page comes from a dataset or a `dash.calc`; don't hard-code figures in markup. Targets are constants inside the `funnel` calc.
- Colors and spacing are CSS custom properties defined once in `:root`, with dark-mode values.

## Hard constraints
- Never commit `site/data.js`, `site/boxbyte.js`, `site/courier.js`, `site/research.js` or any Zoho credentials to `main`. The `courier-snapshot` branch (orphan, single commit, force-pushed) never carries email addresses, phone numbers or people's names. The `boxbyte-snapshot` branch (orphan, single commit, force-pushed) holds the Box Byte batch file; it never carries LinkedIn/Sales Navigator URLs or review notes. The `research-snapshot` branch (orphan, single commit, force-pushed) carries only public professional details (name, title, company, LinkedIn profile, source); never emails, phone numbers or addresses. Secrets live in GitHub Actions secrets only. The `data-snapshot` branch (orphan, single commit, force-pushed) is a stopgap used only while the secrets are missing.
- Meetings with no linked contact are published without titles (`redact_unlinked_meetings`); the calendar includes personal events.
- The Zoho client is read-only (`ZohoCRM.coql.READ,ZohoCRM.modules.READ`). Don't add write scopes.
- The site is public; planned next step is passcode encryption of `data.js` in the workflow.
- Dates display in Central time (CT); the refresh schedule is stated in IST.
- US spelling throughout.
